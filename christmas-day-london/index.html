Christmas Day in LondonFriday 25 December 2026

LONDON25·XII·26

The Christmas markets are all shut on the 25th, but it's the one day you get London almost to yourself, and most of the best bits are free. Here's how I'd spend it.

Mostly freeMarkets closedNo tube or busesDark by 4pm

First, the honest answer

All Christmas markets are closed 25 DEC

Winter Wonderland, Southbank, Leicester Square, all of them. They're back on the 26th, so do the markets on the 24th or 26th.

Before you go

  • 🚫
    No tube, buses or trains at allStay somewhere central so you can walk. Otherwise it's Uber or a taxi (expect surge prices), or a Santander bike.
  • 🛒
    Stock up on the 24thMost shops and supermarkets are shut. Chinatown and a few cafes near Leicester Square stay open if you need a bite.
  • 🧣
    Dress warm, plan for the darkLayers, gloves and a hat. It's dark by about 4pm.
  • 🔋
    Carry a power bankYou'll take a lot of photos.

Free, and only in Londonall on foot · costs nothing

  1. Before 10am
    Empty LondonOxford Street, Regent Street, Piccadilly Circus and Covent Garden with nobody in them. Side streets are the emptiest, and Embankment gives a great London Eye and Big Ben view. Busier from about 11am.
  2. 9am
    Peter Pan Cup, Hyde ParkSanta-hat swimmers jump into the freezing Serpentine for a race held since 1864. Very British, very funny, free to watch.
  3. Late morning
    St Paul's Cathedral choirChristmas Day services with no ticket needed.
  4. Midday
    Royal Park walkFrom Buckingham Palace down The Mall to Trafalgar Square, with hardly any cars.
  5. Afternoon
    The famous trees and windowsThe giant Norwegian tree in Trafalgar Square, Covent Garden's decorations, and the lit windows at Liberty, Hamleys, Selfridges and Fortnum & Mason.
  6. Insta stop
    The Churchill Arms, KensingtonA pub covered in Christmas trees and thousands of lights. Go for the photo.
  7. From 4pm
    The Christmas lightsRegent Street's angels, Oxford Street, Bond Street, Carnaby Street and Covent Garden, all lit up with almost no one around.

Worth paying foroptional · book ahead

  • 🚢
    Thames cruiseOne hour past Tower Bridge and the Tower of London, from about £32.
  • ⛸️
    Ice skating at JW3Finchley Road. The only rink open on the day.
  • 🎬
    Christmas movie in a hotel cinemaThe W Hotel in Leicester Square shows Home Alone, Love Actually and Elf.
  • ⛪
    Westminster Abbey on Christmas morningTickets go in minutes, but the morning Golden Tours "Sights and Sounds" coach tour includes the Abbey service.
  • 🪩
    Christmas night partyLA Lounge at Royal Albert Dock, 10pm to 4am, from £10.

More things people love

  • 🚲
    Cycle through empty LondonA Santander or Lime bike past Big Ben, The Mall and Piccadilly with almost no traffic.
  • 🚶‍♀️
    Christmas Day walking tourA Charles Dickens walk from Trafalgar Square at 2pm, or a Samuel Pepys walk based on Christmas morning in 1660.
  • 📸
    Book a photoshootThe easiest day of the year for crowd-free shots at the big landmarks.
  • 🏊‍♀️
    Your own Christmas swimA few outdoor pools open for a couple of hours, like Hampton Pool. Book weeks ahead.
  • ❤️
    Volunteer for a few hoursCrisis at Christmas runs centres for people experiencing homelessness. Sign up well in advance.
  • ☕
    Coffee stopsA few Pret and Caffe Concerto branches usually open in the centre.

Boxing Day (26th)

Save the markets for the 26th

The Christmas markets, Winter Wonderland and the London Eye are all open again.

Have the best Christmas! 🎄 — Pragati
