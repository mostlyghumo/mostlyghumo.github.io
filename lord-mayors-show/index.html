Lord Mayor's ShowSaturday 14 November 2026

LONDON14 · XI · 26

London's medieval parade, free to watch from the street. Bronek Masojada becomes the 698th Lord Mayor, and this year the river pageant is back for the first time since 2017. Here's when to be where, and the quieter spot most people miss.

Where to watchpick by how much crowd you can handle

Temple to Blackfriars QUIET SPOT

On the return leg, 1.15–2.30pm. Less packed than the City streets, and you still see the whole procession. The Embankment works in the same window.

Bank to St Paul's

11am to noon, right at the start. The most atmosphere and the biggest crowds, so arrive early for a spot at the barrier.

River pageant, 8.30–9.30am

The flotilla sails from Westminster down the Thames and through Tower Bridge to HMS President. Watch from the riverside before the procession starts.

Before you go

  • 🚇
    Bank or St Paul's tubeFarringdon (Elizabeth line) is also well placed. No cars or buses into the City on the day, so take the tube, a river bus or walk.
  • 🧥
    Dress warm and waterproofComfortable shoes too. You'll stand for over an hour, with around 350,000 people expected.
  • 📖
    £5 programme, cash onlySold on the day by Scout and Guide volunteers.
  • 🎆
    No fireworksThe evening display was dropped in 2015 and hasn't come back.
  • ⛪
    After the paradeSt Paul's Cathedral floor is free to enter until 4.30pm. Dome Galleries tickets are £13.50.
